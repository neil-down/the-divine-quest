# The Divine Quest - A Journey Through Faith and Reason

## Overview

**The Divine Quest** is an immersive theological RPG that explores the profound questions of faith, wisdom, and compassion through meaningful choices and rich narrative. This game invites players to embark on a spiritual journey where every decision shapes not just their character's destiny, but their understanding of the divine.

## Game Features

### Core Mechanics
- **Three Primary Attributes**: Faith, Wisdom, and Compassion
- **Branching Narrative**: Multiple paths with theological depth
- **Dynamic Choices**: Every decision impacts your spiritual development
- **Achievement System**: Unlock special titles based on your spiritual growth
- **Save/Load System**: Preserve your spiritual journey across sessions

### Theological Themes
- **The Nature of Faith**: Explore different approaches to divine connection
- **Wisdom and Knowledge**: Balance between rational inquiry and spiritual insight
- **Compassion in Action**: Service as a path to understanding
- **Mystical Experience**: Direct encounters with the divine
- **Community and Service**: The role of relationships in spiritual growth

## How to Play

### Starting Your Journey
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Read the opening narrative carefully
3. Choose your initial path from the three options:
   - **Path of Devotion**: Embrace faith through trust and surrender
   - **Path of Scholarship**: Seek understanding through study and reason
   - **Path of Service**: Find divinity through compassion and action

### Making Choices
- Each choice affects your three core attributes
- Consider how each option aligns with your spiritual values
- There are no "wrong" choices - every path offers unique insights
- Your attributes unlock different achievements and story branches

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Ctrl+S**: Save your game
- **Ctrl+L**: Load a saved game
- **Ctrl+R**: Reset and start a new journey

## Game Structure

### Chapters
1. **The Crossroads of Destiny** - Your spiritual awakening
2. **The Garden of Contemplation** - Learning through meditation and wisdom
3. **The Library of Eternal Wisdom** - Exploring sacred texts and philosophy
4. **The Village of Need** - Putting faith into compassionate action
5. **The Summit of Revelation** - Ultimate spiritual understanding
6. **The Eternal Journey** - The beginning of deeper wisdom

### Attributes Explained

#### Faith (Blue)
- Represents trust in the divine and spiritual conviction
- Increased through prayer, meditation, and surrender
- Essential for mystical experiences and divine encounters

#### Wisdom (Yellow)
- Symbolizes theological understanding and philosophical insight
- Gained through study, contemplation, and intellectual inquiry
- Unlocks deeper interpretations of sacred texts

#### Compassion (Green)
- Embodies love for all beings and service to humanity
- Developed through helping others and empathetic action
- The practical expression of divine love in the world

## Achievements

### Balanced Achievements
- **Enlightened Master**: Achieve 80+ in all three attributes
- Represents perfect spiritual balance

### Focused Achievements
- **True Believer**: 90+ Faith
- **Divine Scholar**: 90+ Wisdom
- **Compassionate Heart**: 90+ Compassion
